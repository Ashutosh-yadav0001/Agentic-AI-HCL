HCLTech Agentic AI Hackathon FAQ

June 8 \- June 30, 2026

**What is a hackathon?** | *Hack \+ Marathon \= Hackathon* |

A hackathon is an intense and fun collaborative marathon where “hack” means to create something entirely new from things that already exist – think “MacGyver.” Developers, designers, and idea generators (participants) are joined by technology partners for a brief period of time – in this case, 24 hours – to develop amazing new products or services in response to challenges posed by the hackathon sponsors.

**Do I have to know how to code to join?** 

Not all participants need to be developers, but you will need to demo a prototype of your idea. So, forming a team that accounts for design, UX and coding is a great way to go. You will have an opportunity to do this the morning of the hackathon, or you may want to recruit colleagues with a broad range of skills to register before the hackathon.

**How are teams formed?**

Teams can be formed ahead of time if you are coming to the hackathon with people you know, or virtually using the Slack channel and team formation webinar happening before the hackathon. Participants are also welcome to participate individually.

**What is the allowable team size?**

Teams may have 1-5 members, but no matter the number of teammates, make sure to cover all hats; development, design (UI/UX), and pitch. 1 person can absolutely wear several hats\!

**What are the challenges?** 

\[Track 1\] Agents/Agentic workflows for industry/business transformation: 

This track invites you to reimagine how industries operate by building Agents that augment, automate and accelerate critical value chains. From supply chain and manufacturing to healthcare, banking, telecom and energy, the focus is on creating agents or agentic workflows that deliver tangible business impact. 

\[Track 2\] Agents/Agentic workflows for HCLTech internal operations: 

This track focuses on transforming how HCLTech works. Participants are encouraged to design agents or agentic solutions that streamline and enhance internal functions, including IT, HR, Finance, Legal, Sales, Marketing, procurement and more. The goal is to unlock measurable gains in productivity, efficiency, and scalability by creating smarter workflows that elevate employee experience and deliver sustained enterprise value.

**What is the required submission criteria?**

A completed project page on the event platform. All required fields need to be submitted and the project page needs to be published before the deadline. 

**What if I don’t finish my project?**

Totally okay. Hackathons are about progress, not perfection. Even a rough prototype or strong idea pitch is valuable.

They are forthcoming and will be revealed in the coming days\!
